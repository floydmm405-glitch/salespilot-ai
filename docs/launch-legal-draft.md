# SalesPilot AI — legal drafts (not approved for publication)

## Privacy notice draft
SalesPilot AI provides lead management and AI-assisted sales suggestions. Account information, lead records, follow-up dates, AI summaries and analysis history are processed to provide the service. Customers must have a lawful basis to enter third-party information. Do not submit personal or confidential information in AI summaries. AI summaries are sent to Groq for analysis. Supabase provides database and authentication services; Render hosts the application. Confirm provider retention, international transfers and contractual safeguards before launch.

For account data the operator acts as controller; for customer lead data the operator's role and processing obligations must be documented in a data processing agreement where applicable. Specify lawful bases, retention periods, access, correction, deletion, portability, objection and complaint procedures and contact details before publication.

## Terms draft
Users must have authority to upload data, protect their accounts and comply with applicable laws. AI suggestions may be inaccurate and must be reviewed before use. The free plan currently has ten AI attempts per UTC day. Pro is planned and not available for purchase. Paid terms must specify price including applicable taxes, renewal, cancellation, refunds and mandatory consumer rights before checkout. No provision should exclude mandatory statutory rights.
